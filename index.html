Killers Pool - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy applies to the Killers Pool apps for iPhone, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Android ("the app"), published by Garry Ryan ("I", "me").

The short version

The app does not collect, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your own device (and, for the Apple Watch app, on your paired watch):

  • Player names and lives that you type in
  • Game settings and saved game history

This information is never sent to me or to any server. It stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app or use the app's reset options.

Information I do not collect

I do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data. The app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Spoken announcements

The app can read out announcements, such as whose turn it is, using your device's built-in text-to-speech. This happens on your device. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

In-app purchase

The app offers an optional one-time "Pro Upgrade" that removes the player limit. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I do not receive your payment details. I only receive confirmation from the store that the purchase was made, which the store provides to the app on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
